Teklora Vision 2026–2035

Our Take on the Future of Technology

Where is digital software heading over the next decade? Here is Teklora's engineering thesis on the 3 fundamental shifts defining tomorrow's technology landscape.

1. Sovereign & Localized AI

Generic centralized AI will be replaced by air-gapped, domain-specific AI models running locally in Africa and regional hubs to preserve data privacy and cultural nuance.

2. Post-Quantum Zero Trust

As quantum decryption matures, standard RSA/ECC encryption will fail. All enterprise databases will migrate to lattice-based post-quantum cryptography.

3. The Spatial Web OS

2D flat web pages will evolve into persistent 3D spatial operating environments accessible inside any browser, merging video, collaboration, and documents into spatial rooms.
