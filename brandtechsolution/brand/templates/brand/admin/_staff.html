How access works
Owner
A superuser. Holds every capability and can manage other owners. Only set in the Django admin, never here.
Administrator
Holds Manage staff and roles, so can invite people and change roles. Can only grant what they hold themselves.
Member
Staff with at least one capability from their roles, such as writing blog posts or sending campaigns.
No access
Staff account with no roles. They can sign in but the panel is empty until you give them a role.
