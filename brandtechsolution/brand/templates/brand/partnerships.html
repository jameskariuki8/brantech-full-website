EduShare Zero-Rating Alliance

Partner with us to zero-rate syllabus access across Kenya and Africa.

EduShare caches decentralized university syllabus content. While currently standard-rated, we invite telcos (Safaricom, Airtel, MTN), governments, and philanthropies to partner with us to sponsor reverse-billing and make EduShare fully zero-rated for students.

Key Value & Mutual Benefits

  • Eliminate student mobile data costs across all 47 Kenyan counties and Africa
  • Verified ESG impact data and prominent institutional co-branding

Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)

Touching Children's Lives & Spreading the Gospel

At Teklora, engineering is fundamentally an act of service. Our CSR mandate is purposefully dedicated to organisations touching the lives of children and ministries spreading the Gospel of Jesus Christ. In addition, while EduShare is not currently zero-rated, we actively invite partners and telcos to collaborate with us to make it zero-rated across Kenya and the African continent.

Touching Children's Lives

Orphanages & Vulnerable Care

Providing direct technical backing, safe computer spaces, learning hardware, and educational mentorship to children's homes, rescue shelters, and vulnerable boys and girls across Kenya.

Mandate: Child Upliftment

Spreading the Gospel

Churches & Evangelistic Missions

Equipping churches, missionary organisations, and evangelical outreaches with pro-bono portals, sermon streaming systems, and congregation communication tools (DOMMaterdei) to proclaim Christ.

Mission: Continental Evangelism

EduShare Zero-Rating Drive

Not Zero-Rated Yet • Partner With Us

EduShare is currently not zero-rated. We invite telcos (Safaricom, Airtel, MTN) and foundations to partner with us to sponsor student data and zero-rate syllabus access across Kenya and all of Africa.

Goal: 100% Free Data for Learners
