Privacy Commitment Summary

Teklora Solutions Ltd ("Teklora", "we", "us", or "our") respects your fundamental right to data privacy. We comply strictly with the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 (KDPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR), and global data privacy standards. We process personal data solely for explicit, lawful, and transparent purposes. We never sell your personal data to third parties.

1. Data Controller Identification

Teklora Solutions Ltd operates as the primary Data Controller for personal data processed through our website (brantechsolutions.co), software applications, API services, and enterprise digital infrastructures.

2. Categories of Information We Collect

Depending on your interaction with Teklora's services, we collect and process the following categories of data:

  • Direct Identification Data: Full name, professional email address, phone number, organization name, and billing details provided during consultation booking or contact forms.
  • Technical & System Telemetry: Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type, operating system, device fingerprint, geographic location (country/city level), referrers, and page interaction metrics.
  • Client Project & Account Data: Authentication credentials, API keys, support tickets, project requirements, and technical specifications submitted to our web platforms.
  • Communication Records: Transcripts of emails, chat assistant interactions, and formal inquiries submitted to our team.

3. Lawful Bases for Processing

In accordance with Section 30 of the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 and Article 6 of the EU GDPR, Teklora processes your personal data only under valid legal grounds:

Contractual Necessity

Processing necessary to deliver software development, execute Statements of Work (SOW), or fulfill consultation requests.

Explicit Consent

Where you voluntarily subscribe to technical updates, request direct communication, or enable non-essential cookies.

Legitimate Interests

Enhancing infrastructure cybersecurity, monitoring platform integrity, preventing fraud, and optimizing system performance.

Legal Obligations

Compliance with statutory tax laws, accounting audits, court orders, or law enforcement mandates.

4. Third-Party Sub-Processors & Data Sharing

Teklora does not rent, sell, or trade personal information to third-party advertisers. Data is shared exclusively with vetted sub-processors operating under strict Data Processing Agreements (DPAs):

  • Cloud Infrastructure Providers: Amazon Web Services (AWS) and Google Cloud Platform (GCP) for secure server hosting and database storage under encrypted protocols.
  • Transactional Messaging Providers: Vetted SMTP email services utilized strictly for administrative alerts, account verification, and service updates.
  • Statutory Authorities: Disclosures mandated by law, court order, or regulatory authorities in Kenya or applicable international jurisdictions.

5. Data Security Standards & Retention

We implement rigorous technical and organizational measures to safeguard your information against unauthorized access, destruction, loss, or disclosure:

  • Encryption Standards: All data in transit is protected using TLS 1.3 encryption, and data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 standards.
  • Access Controls: Role-based access control (RBAC) restricts internal employee access on a strict need-to-know basis.
  • Retention Policy: Personal data is retained only for the duration required to fulfill contractual obligations or as required by applicable statutory accounting laws (typically 7 years for financial records). Technical logs are automatically purged after 90 days.

6. Your Statutory Rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 and EU GDPR, data subjects possess the following enforceable rights:

Right of Access: Obtain confirmation and copy of your personal data held by us.
Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
Right to Erasure ("To Be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your data where legal grounds apply.
Right to Data Portability: Receive data in a structured, machine-readable format.
Right to Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct communications.
Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior lawful processing.

To exercise any of these statutory rights, please submit a formal Data Subject Access Request (DSAR) to teklorasolutionsltd@gamil.com. Requests will be acknowledged within 48 hours and fulfilled within 30 statutory days.
